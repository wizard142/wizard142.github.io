Purpose and access

Wizzy Rant Studio is an owner-operated production tool for the Wizzy Rant YouTube channel. These public pages explain the application and its data practices; they do not offer public registration or account access.

Only the channel owner or a person explicitly authorized by the owner may configure the private workflow, grant account access or publish through the tool. Google-account access requires Google's consent process and remains subject to the permissions actually granted.

Content and publishing

The owner is responsible for reviewing scripts, factual claims, media rights, disclosures, upload settings and the finished videos. AI-generated editorial work can contain errors. The workflow may skip a story, stop at a quality gate or fail to complete a scheduled upload.

The tool must not be used to fabricate news, publish an unconfirmed death as fact, impersonate another person, upload material without the necessary rights, or evade platform restrictions. Source availability does not itself grant permission to reuse a source's images or videos.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. Its use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and applicable Google API policies. Authorization can be revoked through Google Account permissions. The tool does not bypass account verification, API quotas or platform publishing restrictions.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes the account data accessed, how it is used and stored, the service providers involved, and the owner's deletion controls.

Availability and service limits

Operation depends on the owner's subscriptions, included service quotas, account permissions and hosting availability. Scheduled execution and successful publication are not guaranteed. The tool is configured to stop rather than fall back to paid AI APIs when its subscription or authentication limits are reached.

These public information pages do not sell subscriptions, process payments or request payment details.

Support

For support concerning the production tool or its data, use the support email displayed on the Wizzy Rant Studio Google consent screen.
