A studio for stories worth talking about.

Wizzy Rant Studio is the channel owner's private production tool for short-form news commentary, covering entertainment, sport, technology and extraordinary human stories.

It is designed to research public reporting, help write original commentary, create narrated videos with gameplay or approved visuals, and prepare uploads to the owner's YouTube channel. Roblox is a visual format and an optional topic.

For the channel owner. There is no public account-registration or Google sign-in service on this website. YouTube access is granted separately through Google's consent screen for the owner's own channel.
01 / Research

Collect public reports and check evidence. Weak or unconfirmed stories can be skipped.

02 / Produce

Assist with original scripts, narration, captions and appropriate visuals. The owner remains responsible for the output.

03 / Prepare

Upload through the YouTube API with the owner's authorization. Private testing and public scheduling have separate controls.

Why YouTube permissions are requested

The uploader requests permission to upload videos, read the authorized channel's details and upload status, and read its aggregated performance reports. Credentials and operational records are kept in the owner's private GitHub automation repository.

Codex assists with public-source research and editorial writing. Google-account analytics remain in private storage and local reports; they are not sent to Codex.
